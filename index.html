Desktop App

One dashboard for your own accounts.

Tyrone Command Center is a desktop application that lets you connect your own YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram accounts through each platform's official sign-in, and see them in one place — on your own computer.

Your accounts, your device

Every connection goes through the platform's own official OAuth sign-in page. Tyrone never asks for your password, and access tokens are encrypted and stored locally on your machine — not on any server we run.

Local AI assistant

An optional local voice/command assistant and a personal notes ("Second Brain") feature run entirely on your device, reading and writing plain Markdown files you control.

Multi-account, no lock-in

Connect as many accounts on a given platform as you like. Disconnect or remove any of them at any time — disconnecting revokes access with the platform directly.

Built by one developer

Tyrone Command Center is an independent, single-developer desktop project — not a company product, and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or verified by TikTok, YouTube/Google, or Meta.

What Tyrone Command Center is not

Tyrone Command Center is not an official TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram product. It is not partnered with or endorsed by any of those platforms. It does not guarantee account safety, and it does not attempt to bypass, evade, or defeat any platform's spam detection, rate limits, or enforcement systems. It uses each platform's official developer API under that platform's own terms.
